# Empathic Receiving

## Core idea
Empathy is a respectful understanding of what others are experiencing, requiring us to empty our minds and listen with our whole being
Instead of offering advice, reassurance, or explanations, we focus full attention on the other person's observations, feelings, needs, and requests
This quality of presence allows others to fully explore their interior selves
We know a speaker has received enough empathy when we sense a release of tension or the flow of words comes to a halt

## Use in EchoRole
Use this card when a user needs help slowing down the conflict, naming what is happening, and choosing a more constructive communication move.

## Suggested move
Help the user translate emotional reaction into a specific, observable, and actionable response.

## Avoid
- Treating this card as a fixed script.
- Giving the user a single correct answer.
- Ignoring the role, relationship type, and current turn situation.
