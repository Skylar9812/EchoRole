# Expressing Appreciation

## Core idea
Expressions of appreciation in NVC are intended purely to celebrate how our lives have been enriched, not to manipulate or reward behavior
A full appreciation includes three components: 1) the specific actions that contributed to our well-being; 2) the particular needs of ours that were fulfilled; and 3) the pleasurable feelings engendered by that fulfillment
This allows the recipient to receive the appreciation without feelings of superiority or false humility

## Use in EchoRole
Use this card when a user needs help slowing down the conflict, naming what is happening, and choosing a more constructive communication move.

## Suggested move
Help the user translate emotional reaction into a specific, observable, and actionable response.

## Avoid
- Treating this card as a fixed script.
- Giving the user a single correct answer.
- Ignoring the role, relationship type, and current turn situation.
