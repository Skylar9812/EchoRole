# Connecting Feelings to Needs

## Core idea
The third component is acknowledging the needs at the root of our feelings. While others' actions may be a stimulus for our feelings, they are never the cause; our feelings result from our own needs, values, and expectations
All judgments, criticisms, and diagnoses of others are actually alienated expressions of our own unmet needs
Directly connecting your feelings to your needs (e.g., "I feel frustrated because I need more support") makes it easier for others to respond compassionately

## Use in EchoRole
Use this card when a user needs help slowing down the conflict, naming what is happening, and choosing a more constructive communication move.

## Suggested move
Help the user translate emotional reaction into a specific, observable, and actionable response.

## Avoid
- Treating this card as a fixed script.
- Giving the user a single correct answer.
- Ignoring the role, relationship type, and current turn situation.
