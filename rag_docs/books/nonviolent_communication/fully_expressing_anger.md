# Fully Expressing Anger

## Core idea
In NVC, we never blame others for our anger. The stimulus may be someone else's behavior, but the cause is our own judgmental thinking
To express anger fully, we first stop and breathe to refrain from blaming, then identify the judgmental thoughts in our head, connect them to our own unmet needs, and finally express our feelings and needs to the other person
Often, we must offer empathy to the other person first so they are able to hear us

## Use in EchoRole
Use this card when a user needs help slowing down the conflict, naming what is happening, and choosing a more constructive communication move.

## Suggested move
Help the user translate emotional reaction into a specific, observable, and actionable response.

## Avoid
- Treating this card as a fixed script.
- Giving the user a single correct answer.
- Ignoring the role, relationship type, and current turn situation.
