# Managing Negative Emotionality

## Core idea

Frequent anxiety, anger, pessimism, or irritability can make relationship conflict harder to repair. When people are emotionally reactive, neutral events may feel threatening and conversations can become argumentative.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card when a user repeatedly interprets the other person’s behavior in the worst possible way or escalates quickly during disagreement.

## Suggested move

Help the user pause, name the emotional reaction, test the interpretation, and choose a calmer opening before entering the conversation.

## Example phrases

- “Before responding, ask yourself: what is the most generous plausible explanation?”
- “You can say you are upset without turning the whole conversation into a verdict on the relationship.”
- “Try starting with the specific event, not the global conclusion.”

## Avoid

- Invalidating the user’s emotion.
- Telling the user to simply be positive.
- Ignoring legitimate harm or repeated disrespect.
