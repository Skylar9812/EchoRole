# Reducing Technoference and Phubbing

## Core idea

Device interruptions can make people feel ignored or devalued. Even when no harm is intended, looking at a phone during a conversation may signal disinterest and reduce the quality of connection.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card when a conflict involves phone use, divided attention, slow responses, ignored messages, or feeling mentally absent during time together.

## Suggested move

Coach the user to name the impact of device distraction and propose a concrete boundary for focused time, instead of accusing the other person of not caring.

## Example phrases

- “When we are talking and the phone keeps interrupting, I feel less important. Could we put our phones away for ten minutes?”
- “I know you may not mean to ignore me, but I notice I shut down when I feel like I’m competing with your phone.”
- “Can we agree on a phone-free moment when the conversation is important?”

## Avoid

- Equating phone use with lack of love or respect in every case.
- Using digital boundaries as surveillance or control.
- Starting with accusation instead of impact.
