# Self-Esteem as a Sociometer

## Core idea

Self-esteem can function like a social gauge: it rises when people feel valued and drops when they feel excluded or disregarded. This can make rejection cues feel especially powerful, even when the situation is ambiguous.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card when a user’s self-worth seems tied to whether another person responds, approves, invites, or reassures them.

## Suggested move

Validate the sting of perceived rejection, then help the user distinguish between a social signal, an interpretation, and a direct conversation they can have.

## Example phrases

- “Feeling disregarded can hit your self-worth, even before you know what the other person meant.”
- “Let’s separate what happened from what it made you fear about yourself.”
- “You can ask for reassurance without treating one delayed response as proof that you are unwanted.”

## Avoid

- Mocking sensitivity to rejection.
- Treating every drop in self-esteem as evidence that the relationship is unsafe.
- Making the other person solely responsible for the user’s self-worth.
