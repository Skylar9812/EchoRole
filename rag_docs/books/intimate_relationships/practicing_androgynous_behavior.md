# Practicing Androgynous Behavior

## Core idea

Healthy communication often requires both assertiveness and warmth. Rigid gender-role expectations can limit people’s ability to solve problems, offer care, negotiate boundaries, or ask for support.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card when a user believes they must choose between being strong and being caring, or between being agreeable and being direct.

## Suggested move

Coach the user to combine clarity with sensitivity: state the issue directly while still acknowledging the other person’s feelings and needs.

## Example phrases

- “You can be firm without being cold.”
- “You can show care without giving up your boundary.”
- “Try combining a clear request with an empathic opening.”

## Avoid

- Framing assertiveness and empathy as opposites.
- Reinforcing stereotypes about how men, women, parents, partners, or managers should communicate.
- Making the user overcorrect into harshness or passivity.
