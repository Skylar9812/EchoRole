# Attachment Style Optimization

## Core idea

People differ in how they respond to closeness and insecurity. Some fear abandonment, some avoid intimacy, and some are comfortable with closeness. These patterns can be stable, but they are learned patterns and can shift through safer relational experiences.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card when a user reacts with jealousy, withdrawal, clinginess, suspicion, or emotional shutdown. It helps the AI Coach discuss patterns without diagnosing the user.

## Suggested move

Help the user notice their protective pattern, then choose a communication move that creates safety rather than intensifying pursuit or distance.

## Example phrases

- “When you feel insecure, do you tend to chase, withdraw, test, or accuse?”
- “You can name the fear without making the other person responsible for fixing all of it.”
- “Try asking for reassurance directly instead of testing whether they care.”

## Avoid

- Labeling someone as an attachment type as if it is a diagnosis.
- Using attachment language to excuse harmful behavior.
- Assuming the other person’s intention without checking.
