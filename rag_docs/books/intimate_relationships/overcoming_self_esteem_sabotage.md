# Overcoming Self-Esteem Sabotage

## Core idea

When people doubt their own worth, they may underestimate a partner’s care and interpret conflict as rejection. To protect themselves, they may distance, act cold, or test the relationship, which can create the rejection they feared.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card in romantic scenarios where insecurity leads to defensive distancing, accusation, testing, or refusing repair attempts.

## Suggested move

Coach the user to replace testing behavior with a vulnerable, specific request for reassurance or clarity.

## Example phrases

- “Part of me is afraid this means you don’t care, and I do not want to react by pushing you away.”
- “I want to ask directly instead of testing you: do you still want to work through this with me?”
- “I notice I get cold when I feel insecure. I am trying to say that I need reassurance instead.”

## Avoid

- Encouraging reassurance that removes all responsibility from the insecure partner.
- Suggesting manipulation tests.
- Treating insecurity as proof that the partner has done something wrong.
