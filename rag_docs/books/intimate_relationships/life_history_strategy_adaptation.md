# Life History Strategy Adaptation

## Core idea

People’s early environments can shape expectations about safety, stability, trust, and long-term planning. A person raised in unpredictability may approach relationships differently from someone raised with more reliable support.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card carefully when the user is trying to understand why people differ in commitment pace, trust, risk tolerance, or long-term planning.

## Suggested move

Encourage curiosity about background and learned expectations without reducing the person to their past. Focus on how two people can discuss safety, reliability, and future expectations now.

## Example phrases

- “Your backgrounds may have taught you different assumptions about stability and trust.”
- “Instead of judging the difference, try asking what each of you learned to expect from close relationships.”
- “What would make the future feel more predictable and safe for both of you?”

## Avoid

- Making deterministic claims about someone based on upbringing.
- Using family background as an accusation.
- Turning a communication tool into a psychological diagnosis.
