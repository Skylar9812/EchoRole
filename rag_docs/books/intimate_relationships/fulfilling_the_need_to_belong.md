# Fulfilling the Need to Belong

## Core idea

People have a strong need for close social connection. A few high-quality relationships can matter more than many shallow ties. When belonging is threatened, people may feel anxious, lonely, defensive, or unusually sensitive to rejection.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card when the user’s reaction may be intensified by loneliness, isolation, or fear of exclusion. It helps the AI Coach validate the need for connection while still encouraging clear communication.

## Suggested move

Help the user separate the need for connection from the specific strategy they are using to get it. Encourage a request for closeness that is direct rather than controlling or self-protective.

## Example phrases

- “It makes sense that feeling disconnected would hurt. The next step is to ask for connection in a clear way.”
- “You may not need many people to understand you, but you do need reliable closeness somewhere.”
- “Try naming the need for connection without blaming the other person for not meeting it perfectly.”

## Avoid

- Minimizing loneliness as neediness.
- Encouraging dependence on one person for all belonging needs.
- Turning the desire for closeness into pressure or guilt.
