# Evolutionary Mate Selection Strategies

## Core idea

Some mate preferences may be influenced by reproductive and investment pressures, but long-term relationship satisfaction depends strongly on warmth, kindness, reliability, and emotional stability. Biological explanations should not be used to justify stereotyping or disrespect.

## Use in EchoRole

Use this card only when discussing partner expectations, long-term compatibility, or why people may prioritize different traits in dating. Keep the coaching grounded in communication and values rather than deterministic claims.

## Suggested move

Help the user identify the traits they actually need for a healthy relationship: warmth, fairness, trustworthiness, emotional steadiness, shared values, and mutual respect.

## Example phrases

- “Beyond attraction or status, what traits would make this relationship safe and sustainable?”
- “Try asking whether this person is kind, reliable, and emotionally steady when things are difficult.”
- “Long-term compatibility is not just about preference; it is about how two people treat each other under stress.”

## Avoid

- Using evolutionary ideas to excuse jealousy, control, infidelity, or objectification.
- Making rigid gender assumptions.
- Ignoring the individual person and the actual relationship dynamic.
